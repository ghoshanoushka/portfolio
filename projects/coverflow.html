Coverflow: browse Apple Music on Vision Pro one album at a time

A concept that brings Coverflow back to Apple Music on visionOS. Your albums stand in a row in your room, you flip through them, and playing a song can turn the room into its world.

Course
Intro to Product Management, UMSI
Time
2025
Team
Solo, built on a visionOS kit

My part

  • The idea. This class was the first time I tried the Vision Pro. I wanted to bring back Coverflow, the old way of flipping through album covers, in a space where covers can really stand in front of you.
  • Coverflow in 3D. I designed the row of floating covers and how the library window clears to make room for them.
  • The Coverflow icon. An inactive and an active state for switching views.
  • Immersive mode. When the Formula 1 theme plays, the room turns into the track.
  • The prototype. Every screen and transition in the flow, built on a visionOS UI kit.
