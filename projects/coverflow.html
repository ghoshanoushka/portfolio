Coverflow: browse Apple Music on Vision Pro one album at a time

A concept that brings Coverflow back to Apple Music on visionOS. Your albums stand in a row in your room, you flip through them, and playing a song can turn the room into its world.

Course
Intro to Product Management, UMSI
Time
2025
Team
Solo, built on a visionOS kit

My part

  • The idea. This class was the first time I tried the Vision Pro. Some songs take me somewhere: the Stranger Things theme puts me in the Upside Down, the F1 theme puts me on the track. I wanted listening to feel like that, a more indulgent way to enjoy music. So I brought back Coverflow, the old way of flipping through album covers, and let a song turn the room around you into its world.
  • Coverflow in 3D. I designed the row of floating covers and how the library window clears to make room for them.
  • Immersive mode. When the Formula 1 theme plays, the room turns into the track. The Kids theme from Stranger Things takes you to the Upside Down.
  • The prototype. Every screen and transition in the flow, built on a visionOS UI kit.

Models and textures in the Upside Down: Poly Haven (CC0). A fan homage to Stranger Things (Netflix); not affiliated with the show.
