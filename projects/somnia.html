Somnia: wind down and keep a steady bedtime

A sleep app for college students. It nudges you toward the same bedtime every night, walks you through winding down, and plants a seed in your garden for every calming activity.

Course
Consumer Health Technologies, UMSI
Time
Fall 2025
Team
Anoushka, Ciani, Feyi, Jillian, Tolani

My part

  • Brainstorming. I ran the team's brainstorming and came up with the ideas we built on.
  • The de-stressing activities. I designed the Playground: Capture your moment, Collect your calm and Recharge ritual.
  • Their interactions. The breathing circle, and the timed stretch with its progress bar and cheers.
  • The garden. Our professor asked us to keep the routine from feeling mundane, so I designed a reward: every activity plants a seed, seven seeds grow a flower, and staying consistent unlocks new gardens.
